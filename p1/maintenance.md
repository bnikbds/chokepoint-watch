# Chokepoint Watch — record for 19 November 2026

- The official JMIC vocabulary and selected dated regional rows live in `jmic.js`. The map shows the selected bulletin, not a model prediction. The day count uses UTC calendar dates. An age greater than two days triggers the team's review notice without erasing the documented category. Review age is separate from the threat level.
- To add an edition, open the official regional table, check the issue date and number, match the exact named regions and copy only its explicit labels. Add the entry first in the newest-first register, with source URL, page, publication and check dates. Leave uncovered passages unclassified. Ask a teammate to compare the table with the entry before publishing and keep that dated check. A new document download by itself is insufficient. The traffic and weather job does not review JMIC.
- Fresh traffic is estimated from PortWatch. The public history button opens the monthly series; a partial month is marked with an asterisk. The 28-day change requires two complete, dated windows. A user-imported CSV is marked as such. Rebuilding a draft is deliberate; an incoming traffic refresh must preserve user edits.
- The English edition of 19 November uses the dated three-passage export in `observations-56d.csv` and links the underlying immutable collection. Edition 01 and its two-canal annual series remain in `../p0/`.
- The cost and emissions in the Cape example use declared illustrative inputs. A new run of the example restores those inputs. A carrier quote and a validated emissions factor would replace them in commercial use.
- The usability protocol runs locally in the participant's tab. The participant must transfer the resulting JSON to the team. Keep participant records privately, separate external replies from internal dry runs, record task outcome and quote only with permission. Never commit individual responses to this repository.
- Technical checks: `python -m unittest discover -s tools -p 'test_*.py'`, `node tools/test_p0.cjs`, `node --test tools/test_jmic.cjs`. The optional browser regression in `tools/check_p1_browser.py` uses a local release server, Chromium and Playwright with the archived collection for reproducibility. Its generated responses are QA fixtures.
