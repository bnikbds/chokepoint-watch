Can we promise the Rotterdam delivery date?

For a European SME buying non-perishable components from Asia.

What changed

The 18 November collection still ends on 15 November. Suez averaged 43.7 ships per day and Panama 27.9 over the last 28 days. Total traffic at both canals was up 3.4% on the previous 28 days. This edition adds a passage on the importer’s route: Bab el-Mandeb averaged 27.0 ships per day, down 3.1%. Panama is a comparator.

What this means for the importer

The two passages on the same corridor tell different traffic stories. JMIC’s 15 November table lists Suez as MODERATE and Bab el-Mandeb as SUBSTANTIAL. Those labels also appeared in the 12 November edition. Their date matters: our two-day review interval has elapsed. Ask the carrier to confirm the service, route and expected arrival for this booking.

What to watch next

Check the next PortWatch observation and JMIC bulletin. Compare the carrier’s arrival date against stock cover. In our editable two-container example, an additional 6,000 km at 16 knots adds about 8.4 sailing days, excluding port calls. Use the carrier’s actual schedule and quote before revising a customer promise.
